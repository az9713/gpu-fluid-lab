GPU Fluid Dynamics Lab — validation suite ← back to lab

What is this? Most browser fluid demos only prove that they render. This page proves the solver gets the physics right, by re-running classic experiments from the CFD literature and comparing the numbers — the same way a research code is qualified. Nothing is drawn during the runs (the simulations execute headless on your GPU); each card below shows what is being tested, why it matters, and live progress. The full suite takes roughly 15–25 minutes depending on your GPU. Append ?fast to the URL for a ~4-minute smoke run.

A benchmark passes when the simulated numbers land within the stated tolerance of the published reference. Any failure turns the banner red.
